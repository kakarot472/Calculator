## CONTENT CREATION TIPS FOR BEGINNERS

**Instagram Carousels**
- Use Canva (free) with your brand colors from the website
- Slide 1 is your hook — spend the most time on it
- Always end with a CTA (follow, save, share)

**YouTube Videos**
- Record with your phone if you don't have a camera
- Natural light beats expensive lighting
- Don't re-record for mistakes — edit them out
- Use DaVinci Resolve (free) for editing

**LinkedIn Posts**
- Write like you're texting a smart friend
- No need for fancy language
- First line must hook — it shows before "see more"
- Post in the morning (8–9am works well)

**Medium Articles**
- Repurpose your carousel content into longer form
- Add personal stories — that's what differentiates you
- Use subheadings to break it up

**Website Blog**
- Target beginner keywords: "beginner developer mistakes", "how to learn coding"
- This builds SEO slowly but compounds over time
